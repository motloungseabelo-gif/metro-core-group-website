01 Our company

Many capabilities.
One considered
approach.

Good projects need more than a single skill. They need people who can see the whole picture.

Metro Core Group (Pty) Ltd is a South African multidisciplinary company bringing infrastructure, development, construction, maintenance, management, and supply and delivery under one roof.

We combine practical planning, quality workmanship and reliable procurement to help public and private-sector clients meet their project and operational needs.
